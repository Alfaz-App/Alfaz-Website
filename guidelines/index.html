Safety & Content Moderation

Community Guidelines

Last Updated: November 24, 2026 · Version 1.0 (Production Candidate)

A Sacred Literary Space

Alfaz is dedicated to the timeless beauty of the written word. We hold our community to the highest standards of dignity, mutual respect, and poetic expression.

1. The Four Golden Principles

  • Respect the Word: Treat fellow poets and readers with honor. Constructive poetic critique is welcomed; personal attacks and harassment are strictly prohibited.
  • Authentic Expression: Submit original couplets or clearly attribute public domain inspirations. Do not claim another living poet's work as your own.
  • Zero Obscenity: Poetry exploring sorrow, heartbreak, philosophy, and romance is celebrated; graphic sexual violence, vulgarity, or obscenity has no place in Alfaz.
  • Fair Play in the Arena: Respect the democratic nature of community voting. Vote for verses that move your soul, not because of spam or coordinated manipulation.

2. Strictly Prohibited Content

Any content matching the following categories will be removed immediately, and repeat violations will result in permanent device bans:

  • Hate Speech & Discrimination: Content promoting hatred, violence, or discrimination based on race, religion, nationality, caste, gender, sexual orientation, or disability.
  • Harassment & Defamation: Bullying, targeted intimidation, doxxing, or defamatory statements against any individual or public figure.
  • Self-Harm & Violence: Glorification of self-harm, suicide, or incitement to physical violence.
  • Commercial Spam & Links: Using couplet fields to post affiliate links, betting/gambling promotions, or cryptocurrency schemes.

3. Safety & Reporting Tools

Every reader in Alfaz is equipped with built-in safety controls:

  • In-App Report Button: Tap the Report icon on any couplet card or contest entry. You may select reasons such as Hate Speech, Obscenity, Plagiarism, or Spam.
  • Creator Block: Tap Block Poet on any profile or submission to permanently hide that author's verses from your personal feeds.
  • Automated Pre-Filter: All submitted couplets are analyzed through an on-device content moderation dictionary before entering the community arena.

4. Enforcement & Appeals

Reports are reviewed by human moderators within 24 hours. Content found in violation of these guidelines will be removed from all feeds and the Daily 30 Hall of Fame. If you believe your verse was flagged in error, you may file an appeal by emailing alfazapp@duck.com with the verse text and your pen name.
