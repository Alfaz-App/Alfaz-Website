Designed for Quiet Literary Sanctuary

Every detail of Alfaz is built to protect your reading state. No algorithmic rage-bait, zero pop-up ads, and zero surveillance tracking.

📜

Dual-Script Multilingual Vault

Read couplets in their authentic script (Devanagari Hindi, Romanized Hinglish, and English Stoic/Literary quotes) with effortless 1-tap switching.

🎙️

Dual Master Voice Recitations

Listen to human-voiced master recitations: The Doyen (deep gravitas of Ahmad Faraz) and The Ghazal Queen (melodic grace of Parveen Shakir), streamed in lossless high-fidelity audio.

🎻

Acoustic Ambient Soundscapes

Subtle bansuri, classical sarangi, soothing rain, and acoustic piano stream dynamically from our acoustic cloud catalog and cache on-device for uninterrupted offline immersion.

🛡️

Google Drive Cloud Vault

Connect your personal Google Account to sync your private drafts, liked couplets, and awards directly to your hidden Google Drive AppData folder with 100% user privacy.

🎨

Story Studio & HD Export

Compose your own verses and export crystal-clear 9:16 Instagram Stories and WhatsApp cards in custom visual styles without watermark fees.

🔒

Offline-First Resilience

All 307 canonical verses live inside a local SQLite database. Open Alfaz on a flight or subway—it works instantly with zero internet dependency.
