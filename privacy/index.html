Legal & Privacy Governance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 · Version 1.0 (Production Candidate)

Our Core Privacy Principle

Alfaz is built as a distraction-free, privacy-first sanctuary. We do not sell your data, we do not show third-party behavioral advertisements, and your penned poetry drafts belong exclusively to you.

1. Information We Collect

Alfaz is fundamentally an offline-first application. The majority of your activity remains strictly on your local device:

  • Local On-Device Data (SQLite): Your drafted verses, liked/favorite couplets, custom pen name, chosen avatar style, and reading preferences are stored directly in your device's local database. We have zero access to this unshared data.
  • Voluntary Google Account & Google Drive AppData: If you choose to connect your Google Account for cloud backups, Alfaz utilizes the official Google Drive AppData scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata). Your backup file (alfaz_vault_backup.json) is saved directly into your personal Google Drive storage. We do not store, access, or route your backup through external intermediate servers.
  • Community Arena Submissions: If you voluntarily submit an original couplet to the public "Aaj Ka Shayar" contest, your submitted verse and pen name become visible to other readers for community voting.
  • Technical Crash Diagnostics: We utilize Google Firebase Crashlytics to monitor application stability and resolve crashes. This data is strictly anonymized and includes device model, OS version, and technical stack traces. No personal identifiers or poem contents are included.

2. What We Never Do

  • We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal data or written poetry.
  • We never request invasive device permissions such as Contacts, SMS, precise GPS Location, or Microphone.
  • We never embed third-party advertising tracking SDKs (e.g. Meta Audience Network, ByteDance, AdMob behavioral tracking).

3. Google Drive AppData Privacy & Permissions

When you connect Google Drive in your Poet Profile:

  • The connection operates directly between your device and Google's official OAuth servers.
  • The drive.appdata space is isolated and hidden: other applications cannot access your Alfaz backup, nor does Alfaz have access to your personal Google Drive files (photos, spreadsheets, or documents).
  • You may disconnect your Google Account at any time directly inside the app under Poet Profile → Manage Vault → Disconnect, or by revoking access in your Google Account Security Dashboard.

4. User-Generated Content (UGC) & Safety

Alfaz adheres strictly to Google Play Developer UGC policies:

  • Any user can report offensive or infringing couplets using the in-app Report Couplet sheet on any card.
  • Users can block creators permanently using the Block Poet mechanism under Settings.
  • Reported content is reviewed within 24 hours and removed if found in violation of our Community Guidelines.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Alfaz is intended for general audiences who appreciate literature and poetry. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal data through our community features, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

6. Data Deletion & User Rights

Under GDPR, CCPA, and global privacy standards, you maintain absolute control over your information:

  • Local Cache Wipe: You can wipe all local drafts and favorites at any time by clearing the app data in your Android system settings.
  • Cloud Backup Deletion: Disconnecting your Google Account and deleting the app data removes your Google authorization. You may also delete your alfaz_vault_backup.json from your Google Drive.
  • Community Submission Removal: To request immediate deletion of a previously published contest entry or profile data, email us at alfazapp@duck.com with your pen name or couplet ID.
